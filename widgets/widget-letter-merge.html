The idea behind the game

A merge adds one reusable piece to the set of pieces that can represent a word. Byte pair encoding builds a vocabulary by repeatedly joining the most frequent adjacent pair in its training text.

Byte pair encoding picks the most frequent adjacent pair in its training text at each step. Here, c + o occurs four times in cocoa and cocoon. Joining it saves four tiles and adds the piece “co.” Applying that merge to the unseen word coconut gives co, co, n, u, t.
