Every token keeps some probability, because softmax never gives zero.

What changedGreedy decoding takes the most likely token at each step. Finding the most likely whole sequence is intractable, since the number of sequences grows exponentially, so greedy is the cheap approximation. Beam search keeps the k best partial sequences instead of one.
