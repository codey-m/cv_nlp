The idea behind the game

The camera and microphones each guess the same position. Averaging their accepted guesses is additive fusion. Each minimum-clarity rule switches a source on or off according to its current reading. This is a small example of hard gated fusion.

A higher minimum clarity ignores more readings, including some useful ones. When both reject a reading, the spotlight holds still while Pip keeps moving. The target measures the whole visible act: Pip's centre must stay within 22 stage pixels of the beam's centre for at least 97% of its duration.

The motion, clarity cues and sensor errors are an authored simulation. The spotlight, contributions and score are computed from those inputs. Real multimodal models learn gates from data; here you set two thresholds to explore their effect.
