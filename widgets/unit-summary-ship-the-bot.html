What does it take to ship the bot?

1 · Pretrain

A company wants a support bot that answers from its FAQ.

Its staff have written 2,000 example support chats.

Train it from scratch on those chats.†

The bot would have to learn everything about language from those chats alone.

What changedPretraining learns how language and conversations work from a large general corpus, so the company chats only have to teach the company. Trained on small data alone, a model overfits it.

† Illustrative chats, questions and replies.

Pretraining gives a capable model. Fine-tuning makes it the company's.

But fine-tuning every weight means a full model copy for each company.

Full fine-tuning trains every number in every matrix.

3 · Rephrase

Fine-tuning can teach the bot a new fact.

Does it learn the fact both ways round?

The bot learns one sentence about a refund code.

Fine-tuning data

Training longer on the same sentence teaches the same direction harder.

What changedGeneralization from fine-tuning is spiky. Synthetic data spells out the connections the model would not draw on its own, and behavioral testing checks which ones it learned.

† Illustrative. The lecture shows the same failure with a fact about a film director.

4 · Measure

The bot is trained. Now show that it works.

Training lowered its likelihood loss. Is lower loss a better bot?

Three bots. Which is best?

Bot 3 is bot 1 with half its probability moved onto one nonsense reply.

What changedLikelihood is what we train on, not what we want. Evaluation has to look at what the model writes: forced choices, human ratings, or metrics against reference answers.

Negative log-likelihood on the test chats, in bits per chat, from the lecture's three models. Replies and ratings are illustrative.

5 · Score

Judging replies by hand is slow and costly.

So compare each reply with a reference reply, word by word.

Score each reply by its overlap with the reference.

Reference reply:

Shorter replies risk fewer unmatched words, and precision never asks what was left out.

What changedOnce a metric becomes the target, it stops tracking quality. Systems tuned to BLEU produced translations that were too short. Use more than one measure, and people for the final call.

Precision averages the shares of 1- to 4-word sequences found in the reference, as BLEU does. Recall is the share of the reference's words the reply contains, as ROUGE-1 does.

6 · Serve

The bot passes. Now it answers one customer at a time, fast.

Each new token moves every weight from memory, for about 2 flops per weight.

Is the GPU busy, or waiting?

    A faster GPU barely helps, because the cores are already waiting on memory.

    What changedServing one customer at a time is memory-bound, so moving fewer bytes makes it faster even when the flops stay the same. Weight-only quantization relies on exactly that. The scales move too, so finer blocks save less.
