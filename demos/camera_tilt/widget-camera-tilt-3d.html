Where does the point land in the image?

The camera starts level. The marked point P is 2 units above the ground, 1 unit to the right and 1 unit toward the camera. Before you move anything, decide: as the camera tilts down, will the image of P move up, move down, or stay where it is? Then tilt the camera and check. Drag the scene to look at it from any side. That never changes the picture.
